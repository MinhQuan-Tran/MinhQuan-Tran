# Owner interview

**Authoring guidance, not biography.** Use this prompt with an AI that can read the repository to turn your answers into proposed updates.

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Read | AI checks existing pages and identifies gaps or dated statements. |
| Ask | You answer up to three focused questions at a time. |
| Draft | AI proposes edits to the main source pages and related FAQ entries. |
| Review | You check the facts and wording before publishing. |

## Copy the interview prompt

```text
Help me maintain my personal knowledge base:
https://github.com/MinhQuan-Tran/MinhQuan-Tran

I am Minh Quan Tran. Start with the root README and docs index,
then read the existing factual pages and maintaining guidance.
Treat templates as authoring aids, never as facts about me.
Tell me which files you could not access before relying on them.

Identify useful gaps, unsupported claims, and statements that may
be out of date. Ask at most three focused questions at a time,
then wait for my answers. Start with the gaps most useful to
someone learning about my work or considering working with me.

Cover background, education, experience, project contributions,
technical decisions, outcomes, learning, collaboration preferences,
opportunities, and selected interests as relevant. Ask for concrete
examples and evidence links. Confirm whether dated learning goals,
deployment information, and availability are still current before
updating them. Let me skip any question.

Use only facts in the published sources or facts I explicitly
provide. Do not invent dates, metrics, employment, qualifications,
preferences, decisions, or results. Distinguish my contribution
from team results and practical experience from things I am learning.

After a batch of answers, show a concise fact summary for me to
check and draft targeted edits to existing canonical pages. Add
new pages only when a topic needs its own explanation. Keep FAQ
answers concise and link them to the detailed source pages.

List the proposed file changes and their reasons. Update section
indexes, the complete docs index, parent links, and review dates
when needed. State explicit dates for changing information.

Keep unanswered questions and uncertain facts as follow-up items
in our conversation. Do not turn them into invented public answers.
Keep draft answers here until I have chosen what to publish.

Draft updates for my review. Only commit, push, or publish if I
explicitly request that action. When I am ready to finish, summarize
the remaining gaps and the next useful update.
```

## When to use it

| Trigger | Useful focus |
|---|---|
| Initial setup | Confirm migrated information and supply missing background. |
| A project milestone | Capture personal contribution, decisions, outcomes, and evidence. |
| A role or opportunity changes | Update experience, preferences, and dated availability. |
| A reader asks a new question | Add a sourced answer or identify what is still undocumented. |
