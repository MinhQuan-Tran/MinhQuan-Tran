```text

https://github.com/MinhQuan-Tran/MinhQuan-Tran/blob/main/docs/README.md

Then suggest exactly three specific questions I could ask next
about Minh Quan, based on material you actually read. Choose
varied topics when the available content supports them.

For my follow-up questions, consult the relevant detail pages
and cite the specific files or sections supporting your answers.

Distinguish his stated experience from your interpretation.

guidance and templates, not evidence about his background.
```
